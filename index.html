Cinema Visits Report

In January, male and female cinema visits were quite different. Over the first few months, female visits saw a rapid rise, reaching 60% by March, while male visits dropped slightly, a subtle difference that went entirely unnoticed at first.

By June, however, male visits had grown substantially, reaching their peak of 70% and matching female visits. This peak was a hugely significant change.

This trend didn't last. After October, male visits were clearly on the way out, plummeting down to 15% in December. Meanwhile, female attendance showed a steady shift downwards after June, but the habit was already taking hold, remaining relatively high at 60% until the end of the year.
